## ⚡ CALL CENTER CASE STUDY — SSDI & VA OUTBOUND (U.S.)

**TL;DR:** Our Voice-AI beat human reps and generated **$1,174,600** in client revenue in **171 days**—fully **TCPA-compliant**, fully **production** (not a demo).

**Scope & Setup**

* **Traffic:** 137,793 opt-in leads captured via multi-platform ads (~$0.25 CPL).
* **Cadence:** 6 attempts/lead (2× day 1, then every ~26 hours), dayparted for morning answer spikes.
* **Guardrails:** Qualify before transfer, zero “curiosity passes,” transcripts + DNC + quiet hours.
* **Stack:** Live, real call routing; Ringba for buyer delivery; GHL for CRM; full analytics loop.

**Production Throughput**

* **Total dials:** 549,713
* **Total minutes:** 266,433
* **Transfers:** 5,729 total • **Paid qualified transfers:** **691** (SSDI 592, VA Dis 95, VA Appeals 4)
* **AI ops cost:** $36,683.96

**Performance**

* **Client close rate on qualified transfers:** **35%**
* **Closed cases:** **241** (SSDI + VA)
* **Client revenue:** **$1,174,600**
* **Media spend estimate:** ~$34,448 (137,793 × $0.25 CPL)
* **Engine cost (media + ops):** ≈ $71,132 → **~15× ROI** to clients

**AI vs Human (same shop, same buyers)**

* Humans hovered **~18–21%** in the period.
* Our Voice-AI ran **~35%** in the last 30 days and consistently out-converted.

**Why It Won**

* **Speed-to-lead:** calls in minutes, not hours.
* **Relentless follow-up:** cadence never slips.
* **Transfer discipline:** only qualified calls hit the buyer.
* **Dayparting:** staffed into the hours that actually convert.
* **Weekly iteration:** transcripts → fixes → measurable lift.
* **Inbound safety net:** callbacks caught by an inbound agent (extra lift most teams miss).

**Compliance**

* 100% **opt-in** origin, consent artifacts on file, instant DNC honoring, jurisdiction-safe notices, full audit trail.
